## 1. The finding that changes the record

`docs/final-ranking.md` scores **Nullspace 8/30 on originality** — by far its lowest
mark, and the reason it placed 4th instead of 1st. That score rests on a
"Competitive reality" table naming public rivals (DataSheriff, CASCADE, Aegisflow,
Notary, EPISTEME, Groundskeeper, Atlarix, LineageGuard, RationaleOps, Obsel, Lineage
Sentinel, Silent Drift Sentinel, LeakWatch), attributed to a *"Public-entry scan
conducted on 2026-08-07."*

**That scan cannot have happened.** Probed 2026-08-08:

- `datahub.devpost.com/project-gallery` returns, verbatim: *"The hackathon managers
  haven't published this gallery yet, but hang tight!"* There is no public list of
  entries to scan, and there was none on 2026-08-07 either.
- Web search for those project names returns **no DataHub-hackathon result for any of
  them.** The only "Aegisflow" hits are unrelated projects in other hackathons.

**Status: UNVERIFIABLE, not disproven.** The field may well be crowded — 2,865
participants are registered. But nobody can see it, so nobody could have scanned it,
and **an originality score cannot be assigned against rivals that cannot be
observed.** This is the project's own lesson: check that the witness could speak
before trusting a verdict about it.

I recommended two things before this and was wrong both times, in ways worth
recording:

1. **I recommended Join Treaty.** Oscar looked at it running and ruled *"a feature,
   not ambitious enough."* My own one-pager had already conceded it was *"a feature
   of a catalog, not a company"* — I recommended it anyway because it was the thing
   that worked. Verified ≠ worth shipping.
2. **I then proposed "Half-Life"** and pitched *"it's Helicon"* as a selling point.
   Oscar: *"but this is mountain of helicon once again."* Correct. I handed him his
   own existing engine with a new name — and I reached for it because the fabricated
   table had already crossed out the idea that wasn't.
3. **I ruled twice without probing the field**, which is a standing rule of his and
   the direct cause of both errors. The probe took four minutes and overturned the
   premise.
